Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: May 27, 2026

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

At NT Global Hub, accessible from ntglobalhub.com, we strive to ensure complete customer satisfaction with our digital products, services, and corporate solutions.

1. Digital Products & Deliverables

Due to the immediate access and non-returnable nature of digital assets, corporate toolkits, software licenses, and template repositories, sales are generally considered final once the digital access or download link is generated and delivered.

2. Refund Eligibility Criteria

We issue refunds for digital products under the following valid circumstances within 14 days of purchase:

  • Non-Delivery: If technical errors prevent delivery of your digital access link and support cannot resolve it within 48 hours.
  • Major Defects: If the delivered digital product is demonstrably defective or missing core functional components promised in the product description.
  • Duplicate Purchase: Accidental duplicate billing for the exact same digital asset or service.

3. Cancellation Policy

For recurring services or ongoing digital consultations, clients may cancel their service or subscription renewal at any time prior to the next billing cycle by reaching out to our support team.

4. How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund or cancellation request, please contact us with your transaction details:

  • Email Support: contact@ntglobalhub.com
  • Required Info: Order ID, payment proof, and a detailed explanation of the issue.

Our official support desk reviews all requests within 24–48 hours and processes approved refunds directly back to the original payment method.
